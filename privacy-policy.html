1. Overview & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sanskriti Android mobile application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our service on Android devices via the Google Play Store.

Sanskriti is a free-to-use devotional and cultural reading app featuring the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita (all 18 chapters and 700 verses with translations and life lessons), revered Hindu Prayers, Aartis & Chalisas, inspiring Moral Stories of Shri Krishna, Shri Rama, and Shri Hanumanji, and daily wisdom reminders.

✅ No Account Required: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, email, or phone number. Your reading progress, favorites, and preferences remain strictly on your local device.

By installing or using the Sanskriti App, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs & Services

We do not operate backend user databases that collect your personal profile. However, the App integrates standard third-party SDKs and network endpoints that process minimal technical data:

a) Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud detection.
  • IP Address: Used by Google to determine coarse geographical region (country-level) for ad localization.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, impressions, and display errors.

b) App Performance & Crash Diagnostics

  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Aggregated, anonymized performance metrics and stack traces collected via Google Play Services to identify bugs and maintain stability across Android devices.

c) Remote Version Checks (GitHub Gist)

  • IP Address & Request Headers: When the App launches, it makes an encrypted HTTPS GET request to a public configuration file hosted on GitHub Gist to check for app version updates. During this request, GitHub's servers receive and log your device's IP address and standard HTTP request headers in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not store, view, or process this IP address.

AdMob and Google Play Services data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

6. Google AdMob & Advertising

Sanskriti is free to use and supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob. We display:

  • Banner Ads: Small ads displayed at the bottom of specific screens.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads displayed occasionally after completing reading sections.
🛡️ Ad Personalization & Controls: Google AdMob may serve advertisements tailored to your interests using your device's Advertising ID, or contextual ads based on the app's content. You maintain full control over ad personalization and can opt out or reset your Advertising ID anytime via your device settings.

Target Distribution & Regional Compliance

The Sanskriti App is primarily published for and distributed to users in India and global regions where standard AdMob advertising is supported. In jurisdictions requiring explicit consent mechanisms (such as the European Economic Area or the UK), Google AdMob automatically serves non-personalized, contextual advertisements where applicable consent is not obtained.

Controlling Your Advertising ID

You can manage, reset, or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization).

7. Local Daily Wisdom Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules daily local reminders delivering an uplifting Shloka each morning at 8:00 AM.

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your Android device using standard inexact alarm timing (inexactAllowWhileIdle).
  • We do not use exact alarm permissions (such as SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM or USE_EXACT_ALARM).
  • No data is sent to or received from push notification servers.
  • On Android 13+, the App requests the runtime POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission. You may decline or disable notifications at any time in device settings.

8. Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Audio

The audio feature utilizes your Android device's native Text-to-Speech engine (such as Google Speech Services):

  • Text is synthesized locally on your phone. No audio is recorded, uploaded, or transmitted.
  • No microphone permission is required or requested.
  • Audio playback settings (speed, pitch) are saved locally on your device.

9. Sharing & Image Generation

When you share a verse or story card, the image is rendered completely in device memory using Flutter's canvas and passed to Android's native Share Sheet. No images are uploaded to any server, and no sharing activity is tracked by us.

10. AI-Assisted Content Disclosure & Accuracy

⚠️ Transparent Disclosure Regarding Content Generation

In the spirit of complete honesty and transparency, users should be aware that while the verses represent ancient traditional Hindu spiritual heritage:

  • The digital text, verse transliterations, English and Hindi translations, verse explanations, life lesson commentaries, and moral character story adaptations in this App were generated, structured, and translated with the assistance of advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) language models (including Anthropic Claude).
  • Devotional illustrations and character graphics were created using digital and AI-assisted graphic tools.
  • AI-assisted translations and commentaries may occasionally contain nuance variations, grammatical simplifications, or unintentional inaccuracies.
  • The App is intended for personal devotional reflection, inspiration, and educational appreciation; it is not a certified theological authority or a substitute for scholarly critical editions.

13. Your Rights Under India's DPDP Act, 2023

For users residing in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) provides you with statutory rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access Information: You have the right to request a summary of personal data processed about you. Because Sanskriti operates without accounts, logins, or backend databases, no personal profile or user records are maintained on our servers.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: All reading bookmarks, favorites, and settings exist solely within your local device memory. You can erase all data immediately at any time by clearing the app cache/data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Sanskriti > Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the App.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw notification permissions at any time via Android app notification settings, or reset your Advertising ID via Google settings.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: If you have any inquiries, feedback, or complaints regarding your privacy or data handling, you may write directly to our designated Grievance Officer:
    📧 Grievance Officer: appsanskriti@gmail.com
    We acknowledge all grievances promptly and resolve them within 7 business days.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to designate another individual who may exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, in accordance with applicable rules under the DPDP Act.

14. Data Security

  • Encrypted Connections: All network communications (remote version checks and AdMob requests) occur exclusively over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Local Sandbox: On-device settings and bookmarks are protected by the Android operating system's application sandbox security model.
