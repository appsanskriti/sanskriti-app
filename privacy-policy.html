1. Overview & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sanskriti mobile application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our service on Android or iOS devices.

Sanskriti is a free-to-use spiritual and cultural app offering sacred Hindu scriptures (Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata), devotional prayers and aartis, moral stories, and daily wisdom notifications — available in English, Hindi, and Gujarati.

✅ Simple Summary: We do not require you to create an account, enter your name, or provide any personal information. Your reading data stays on your device.

By downloading or using the Sanskriti App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any part, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal, non-identifying information through third-party services only:

a) Usage Analytics (via Google Play Services)

Google Play Services may automatically collect device diagnostics and crash reports including:

  • Device model, operating system version, and language setting
  • App version and crash stack traces (crash reports)
  • General geographic region (country-level, not precise location)
  • Session duration and feature-level interaction (anonymized, aggregate)

This data is collected by Google, not us, and is used to ensure app stability and compatibility.

b) Advertising Data (via Google AdMob)

Google AdMob, our advertising partner, may collect and use the following data to serve personalized or contextual ads:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android; IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address (used to infer country/region — not stored by us)
  • App usage patterns and ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
  • Device identifiers (for ad frequency capping)

This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's policies.

c) Remote Configuration (via GitHub Gist)

At app launch, the App fetches a small configuration file from a public GitHub Gist (HTTPS-secured). This file contains version update information and ad frequency settings. No personal data is transmitted or stored in this request.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect or store any of the following:
  • Your real name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your precise GPS or location data
  • Your photo, camera, or microphone data
  • Your contacts, call logs, or SMS data
  • Financial or payment information of any kind
  • Passwords or authentication tokens
  • Browsing history outside the App
  • Biometric data

There are no user accounts, login systems, or sign-up requirements in the App.

6. Google AdMob & Advertising

Sanskriti is a free app supported by advertisements served via Google AdMob. We display:

  • Banner Ads — small ads shown at the bottom of certain content screens
  • Interstitial Ads — full-screen ads shown periodically (e.g., after reading several shlokas) based on a configurable frequency set via remote config

Your Ad Choices

Google AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. You can limit ad personalization:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Allow Apps to Request to Track (toggle off)

Even with personalization disabled, you may still see contextual ads (not personalized) in the App.

⚠️ EU/EEA Users (GDPR): If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, Google AdMob will only serve non-personalized ads unless you have explicitly provided consent. We currently display non-personalized ads by default for all users.

7. Push Notifications – Daily Wisdom

With your permission, the App schedules daily local push notifications delivering a Bhagavad Gita shloka (verse) each morning for spiritual enrichment.

  • Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using Flutter Local Notifications
  • No notification content or user data is sent to any external server
  • Tapping a notification opens the relevant shloka inside the App
  • You can disable notifications at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Sanskriti > Notifications
✅ The App requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on Android 13+ before scheduling notifications. You can deny this permission and the App will still function fully without notifications.

8. Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Audio

The App includes a text-to-speech engine powered by your device's built-in TTS system (e.g., Google Text-to-Speech on Android) and pre-recorded audio files bundled within the App.

  • TTS processes text locally on your device — no audio is recorded or transmitted
  • No microphone permission is required or requested
  • The App uses wakelock during TTS playback to prevent the screen from turning off
  • TTS speed and preferences are saved locally on your device

The TTS engine installed on your device may be subject to that engine's own privacy policy (e.g., Google's Privacy Policy for Google TTS).

9. Sharing & Social Features

The App allows you to share sacred content as a beautiful image card (PNG). The share card is rendered on your device and contains:

  • The shloka text / story excerpt / prayer name
  • A reference to the source scripture (e.g., "Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2 · Verse 47")
  • Sanskriti app branding and Play Store link

The image is generated entirely in memory on your device and is shared directly to your chosen app (WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.) via Android/iOS Share Sheet.

  • No images are uploaded to any server
  • No shared content is logged or stored by us
  • The receiving app's own privacy policy applies once content is shared

While we make every effort to ensure accuracy, relevance, and respect for sacred traditions:

  • AI-generated content may occasionally contain errors, inaccuracies, or imperfect translations that do not fully represent the original scripture's intended meaning
  • AI-generated or AI-assisted images are for illustrative purposes only and may not be historically or religiously precise
  • The App content is not intended to be a definitive academic or religious authority
  • Sacred text content (original Sanskrit shlokas, Devanagari/Gujarati transliterations) is taken from public domain sources and is not AI-generated

13. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Here is how your data is protected:

  • No personal data on our servers: Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data to our own servers, there is no central database of personal information to be breached.
  • HTTPS-only communication: All network requests made by the App (remote config fetch, AdMob ad requests) are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local-first architecture: Your reading progress, favorites, and preferences are stored locally in an SQLite database on your device, protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.).
  • No third-party analytics SDK: We do not integrate any standalone analytics SDK (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) beyond what Google Play Services provides automatically.
🔒 The safest way to protect your local data is to use your device's built-in screen lock and enable device encryption (enabled by default on modern Android and iOS devices).
