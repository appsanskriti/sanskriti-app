2. Description of the App

Sanskriti is a free, ad-supported spiritual and cultural mobile application offering:

  • Sacred Scriptures: Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata — with Sanskrit shlokas, transliterations, translations, explanations, and life lessons
  • Prayers & Aartis: Daily devotional prayers, stotras, chalisa, and aartis in multiple languages
  • Sacred Stories: Moral and mythological stories of Hindu deities and characters (e.g., Krishna, Ram, Hanuman, Ganesh, Durga, Shiva)
  • Daily Wisdom: Scheduled local notifications with a daily shloka for spiritual reflection
  • Audio Playback: Text-to-speech audio of scriptures, prayers, and stories
  • Multi-Language Support: Content available in English, Hindi, and Gujarati
  • Personalization: Dark mode, reading fonts, background colors, favorites, and bookmarks
  • Sharing: Share sacred verses and stories as image cards to social media or messaging apps

The App is designed for personal, non-commercial, devotional, and educational use only.
