2. Description of the App & Current Content

Sanskriti is a free devotional and educational reading application for Android devices offering:

  • Shrimad Bhagavad Gita: All 18 chapters and 700 verses with original Sanskrit verses, transliterations, English and Hindi translations, verse-by-verse explanations, and practical life lessons.
  • Sacred Prayers & Aartis: Popular devotional hymns including the Hanuman Chalisa, Gayatri Mantra, Om Jai Jagdish Hare, Ganesh Aarti, Shiv Aarti, Durga Aarti, Lakshmi Aarti, Bajrang Baan, and Mahamrityunjaya Mantra.
  • Moral & Character Stories: Inspiring cultural and moral stories centered on revered avatars and figures: Shri Krishna, Shri Rama, and Shri Hanumanji.
  • Today's Wisdom: Locally scheduled morning notifications with daily verses for spiritual contemplation.
  • Reading Customization: Adjustable font sizing, Devanagari and Gujarati typography, light and dark themes, and offline bookmarks/favorites.

The App is intended strictly for personal, educational, devotional, and non-commercial purposes.
